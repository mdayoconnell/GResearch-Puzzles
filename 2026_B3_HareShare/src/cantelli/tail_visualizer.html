Let \(q=(p-1)/2\). The constant \(\beta\) scales the \(m=2\) Fourier branch, while \(\alpha\) scales the nonexact Cantelli branch. The complete candidate bound on integer times \(1\le m\le p-1\) is
\[ F(p,m)\le U_\alpha(p,m):= \begin{cases} q^2, & m=1,\\[2pt] \beta(0.205p^2), & m=2,\\[4pt] \displaystyle \alpha\!\left(q+\frac{(p-1)(p-m-1)}{m+2}\right), & 3\le m<\left\lceil q/2\right\rceil,\\[8pt] 3q-2m, & \left\lceil q/2\right\rceil\le m<q,\\[2pt] q, & q\le m\le p-1. \end{cases} \]
The first, fourth, and fifth branches are exact. The tuner tests the second and third branches against the loaded maxima data.
The theorem preset lets \(0.461\le\alpha\le1\) and \(0.687\le\beta\le1\). These lower endpoints are the current 100-prime smoke-test floors, not yet proofs for every prime.
